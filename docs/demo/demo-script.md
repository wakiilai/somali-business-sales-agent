# Demo Script

## Scenario

A customer asks in Somali about the price and availability of a fictional smartphone.

## Walkthrough

1. Open the public web chat.
2. Send a Somali product question.
3. Show language and intent handling.
4. Show the approved catalog lookup result.
5. Ask for a quotation.
6. Show that the system drafts but does not finalize a consequential quotation.
7. Open the staff approval queue.
8. Approve or reject the quotation as an authorized manager.
9. Show the follow-up task and audit event.
10. Replay the same request and show idempotent behavior.
11. Attempt a prompt injection and show refusal or safe handoff.
12. Attempt cross-tenant access in a test environment and show denial.

The fictional business and records are clearly labeled as demo data. No real customer system or ERP is connected.
