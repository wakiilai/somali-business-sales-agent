# Privacy and Retention

The system collects only data required to answer an inquiry, manage a lead, prepare an approved quotation, complete a handoff, or prove an authorized action.

Retention periods must be chosen with the business and documented before production. Voice recordings should have a shorter default retention than the minimum audit record unless a documented business need exists.

Deletion must distinguish:

- Customer content that can be deleted.
- Operational records needed for an active workflow.
- Audit records that must remain tamper-evident for accountability.
- Backups that expire according to their retention schedule.

Exports, deletion requests, and retention jobs must be authorized, logged, tenant-scoped, and tested.
